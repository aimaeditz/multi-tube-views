Currency Format Formatter

100% Client-Side

Format financial numbers into global international currency styles (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR Lakhs/Crores) and generate formal written number words.

How to Use Currency Format Formatter

1
Enter Amount

Type any integer or decimal currency amount.

2
Select Currency & Locale

Choose USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (₹), JPY (¥), or other global currencies.

3
Copy Formatted Text

Copy formatted currency string or full written words.

Key Features & Privacy

✓ Global Locale Support

Formats with commas, periods, spaces, or South Asian Lakhs/Crores grouping systems.

✓ Amount in Words

Converts raw figures into written English words (e.g., "Five Thousand Two Hundred Dollars").

✓ Accounting & Negatives

Choose standard minus sign or formal accounting parenthesis ($1,200.00).

🔒 Complete Client-Side Isolation
