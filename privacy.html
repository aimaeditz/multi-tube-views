Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview & Architecture

Multi Tube Views ("MTV", "we", "our") is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. The workspace operates entirely within your web browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We do not maintain server-side user accounts, databases, or tracking infrastructure.

2. Data Collection by Multi Tube Views

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign up, or provide email addresses or credentials to use MTV.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not log your IP address, capture your keystrokes, or transmit your entered video URLs to any central server.
  • Local Preferences Only: Harmless user preferences (such as light/dark theme choice, layout columns, and aspect ratio) are stored solely on your device via your browser's localStorage API.

3. Third-Party Media Embeds & Content Providers

When you choose to generate and render an embedded player from third-party services (such as YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Spotify, SoundCloud, or Kick), your browser establishes a direct network connection with the respective platform's servers.

These third-party platforms may collect IP addresses, set cookies, and track media playback according to their own respective privacy policies and terms of service. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the services you interact with:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Vimeo Privacy Policy
  • Twitch / Amazon Privacy Policy
  • Spotify Privacy Policy
  • SoundCloud Privacy Policy

4. AI Prompts & Tools Privacy Disclosures

The AI Prompts & Tools section provides access to prompt engineering templates and links to external AI image tools:

  • Curated Prompt Library: Content is populated cleanly to assist digital creators.
  • Automatic Content Reflection: The section dynamically displays available prompt content without injecting user trackers or analytics.
  • Local Copy Functionality: Individual copy actions execute entirely within your local browser runtime without logging copied text or telemetry.
  • No Server-Side Generation: No artificial or fabricated user profiles or telemetry are generated on our servers.

5. Local Storage Usage

The only local data keys utilized by Multi Tube Views are:

  • mtv_theme: Saves your preference for Light, Dark, or System mode.
  • mtv_density: Saves your display density preference (Comfortable vs. Compact).
  • mtv_motion: Stores reduced-motion accessibility preference.
  • mtv_layout_cols: Stores preferred multi-player grid column count.
  • mtv_aspect_ratio: Stores default aspect ratio preference.

6. Changes to this Policy

Because Multi Tube Views is a static client-side project, any updates to our privacy disclosures will be reflected directly in this document with an updated revision date.
