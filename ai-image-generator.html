Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating with the MTV AI Image Generator.

What is the best way to write prompts for MTV AI?

Be descriptive about the subject, setting, lighting, and camera perspective. Use the built-in Enhance button to automatically append cinematic textural modifiers and balanced lighting keywords.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes. Renders created on MTV AI are yours to download and use across YouTube thumbnails, video assets, websites, and social media channels.

Which aspect ratios are supported?

We support 1:1 (Square), 16:9 (Landscape for YouTube), 9:16 (Vertical for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok), 4:3 (Classic), and 3:4 (Portrait).
